# Coverage Map

This file is the concise view of what the project has examined, what it only suspects exists, and where important gaps remain. It should not become the final taxonomy or a substitute for cycle artifacts.

## Current state

No subject-matter reconnaissance has begun. The areas below come from `outline.md` and the project interview; they are seed hypotheses awaiting independent landscape research.

| Candidate area | Current maturity | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agentic loops and control flow | Seed hypothesis | Likely foundational; boundaries with planning and workflows need testing. |
| Tool interfaces and environmental feedback | Seed hypothesis | Includes tool ergonomics and verification signals. |
| Context construction and lifecycle | Seed hypothesis | Includes budgeting, retrieval, compaction, and long-horizon continuity. |
| Procedural knowledge and skills | Seed hypothesis | Relationship to prompts, retrieval, and memory needs careful separation. |
| Persistent state and memory | Seed hypothesis | Storage is only one part; write, recall, verification, and decay policies matter. |
| Planning, workflows, and delegation | Seed hypothesis | May separate into multiple architectural responsibilities after recon. |
| Evaluation and improvement feedback | Seed hypothesis | Must distinguish in-loop verification from outside-loop harness evaluation. |
| Self-modifying or self-improving harnesses | Seed hypothesis | Frontier area with unusually high freshness and evidence risks. |
| Model selection and routing | Interview addition | Present in scope but underrepresented in the seed outline. |
| Permissions and operational boundaries | Conditional area | Study where it shapes agent behavior or loop architecture, not as general security. |
| Harness observability | Conditional area | Study where it enables diagnosis, evaluation, or improvement. |

## Coverage dimensions for the first recon

The first landscape should sample several agent domains, open and closed systems, recent and foundational sources, production implementations, research prototypes, empirical evaluations, negative results, and materially different control-flow philosophies. These are selection dimensions, not quotas.
