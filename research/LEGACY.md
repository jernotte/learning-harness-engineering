## What is retired

The archive contains the pre-pivot research questions, responsibility taxonomy,
case selection and ordering, source records, case studies, claim ledgers,
provenance packages, syntheses, decisions, curriculum, learning Markdown,
generated sites, and presentation tooling. Their presence and historical review
status do not make them V2 evidence or design authority.

Old internal analyses may serve only as leads. Old factual claims must be
re-established from their underlying evidence before use.

## Selective readmission protocol

Legacy material may enter V2 only when all of the following occur:

1. A maintainer-approved V2 outline creates a concrete question.
2. The primary records why a named archived artifact may answer that question.
3. The underlying source, version, and exact location are reopened.
4. Freshness, applicability, independence, and lineage are reassessed against
   the V2 question.
5. New V2 provenance events and, when referenced, a new V2 source record are
   created.
6. Any material claim receives a new V2 claim ID, exact mappings, primary
   verification, and the review required by the then-current method.
7. The V2 record notes the archived origin so reuse remains visible.

Prior maturity, approval, verification, and claim IDs never transfer. A source
may be readmitted; an old conclusion is never inherited.
