## Review scope and limitation

This ledger covers the context-management synthesis only. It reuses primary-verified locations from four maintainer-reviewed cases and three admitted research records, then adds two primary-verified, topic-specific implementation records for Codex CLI and Letta. Those bounded records are not full case studies and do not become reviewed harness findings. Independent Fable review and maintainer content review remain pending. The artifact is working synthesis, not a reviewed finding or consolidated pattern.
