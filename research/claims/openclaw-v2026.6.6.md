## Revision history

- **2026-07-17 — C003 clarification during maintainer review.** An adversarial review identified that the original explanation did not reconcile the configured `after_agent_dispatch` acknowledgement policy. Primary reinspection showed that the tracker applies that stage on successful middleware-chain return rather than a mechanically observed agent dispatch, while the relevant handlers can return after scheduling process-local buffers. C003 retains its ID because this clarifies the same pre-dispatch durability inference without changing its scope or epistemic kind; the canonical declaration, exact locations, verification notes, and prose attestation were regenerated before promotion.
