# Harness Engineering V2 — Skeleton Pressure Ledger

**Checkpoint:** `V2-SK-CP00`
**Status:** Historical basis for the rejected V2-SK-CP00 portfolio
**Authority:** V2-D012
**Promotion:** None

This ledger records how structural inputs pressure the provisional skeleton
portfolio. It does not count source recurrence as importance, convert a direct
screen into evidence, or allow an agent to change a skeleton between
maintainer checkpoints.

V2-D013 rejected that portfolio's organizing spines. This ledger remains the
auditable record of how checkpoint 00 was formed; it is not the active
vocabulary basis. See
[`conventional-vocabulary-map.md`](conventional-vocabulary-map.md).

The register keeps four epistemic layers separate:

- **Captured input** points to the exact retained local record. Its original
  epistemic limits continue to apply.
- **Basis/status** states whether the row begins from source framing, a prior
  primary synthesis, a deterministic catalog fact, or an unratified screen.
- **Structural pressure** is the checkpoint-00 primary's inference from that
  basis, not a source-native claim.
- **Portfolio response** is a provisional outline proposal awaiting maintainer
  review, never an evidence finding or approved taxonomy.

## Pressure vocabulary

| Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `new_principle` | A genuinely different unit of organization may be needed. |
| `boundary_challenge` | The input exposes a consequential disagreement about membership or system edges. |
| `sequence_challenge` | A dependency or ordering assumption may be wrong. |
| `split_merge_pressure` | Material currently grouped together may need separation, or separated material may need combination. |
| `cross_cutting_pressure` | The material affects several homes and needs an explicit mapping rule. |
| `reinforcement` | The input exercises an existing structural option without establishing its correctness. |
| `no_structural_effect` | The input adds another example but does not change an organizing option. |

These values describe impact on an outline hypothesis. They are not provenance
dispositions or evidence-maturity labels.

## Future batch protocol

For each separately authorized direct-screen batch:

1. retain the existing per-family screening register and provenance boundary;
2. add one pressure row per family using the vocabulary above, including
   `no_structural_effect` when appropriate;
3. separate source-native framing from the primary's structural inference;
4. record which active skeleton or challenger is affected and why;
5. do not revise skeleton prose until the checkpoint boundary; and
6. report both novelty and repetition without treating either as independent
   corroboration.

At a checkpoint, the review packet reports:

- new organizing principles and boundary or sequence challenges;
- splits, merges, or cross-cutting mappings proposed;
- the number and identity of inputs with no structural effect;
- whether the active skeletons remained materially different;
- whether a challenger should enter the active portfolio;
- expected information gain from another screening tranche; and
- any recommendation to continue, reorder, target gaps, or stop.

No numerical novelty threshold alone establishes saturation. The maintainer
decides whether the remaining catalog work is worth its expected effect on the
outline.
